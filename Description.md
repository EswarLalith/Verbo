**Verbo** is an AI-powered multimodal input and prompt optimization platform designed to enhance interactions with Large Language Models (LLMs). It enables users to communicate through both voice and text by converting spoken input into accurate text and intelligently processing typed input. The platform optimizes prompts by removing redundant information, preserving context, and reducing token consumption, resulting in faster response times, lower inference costs, and improved overall efficiency. Built with a focus on scalability and seamless AI integration, Verbo serves as a foundation for developing intelligent voice-enabled applications, AI agents, and conversational systems that deliver accurate, context-aware, and cost-effective AI experiences.
