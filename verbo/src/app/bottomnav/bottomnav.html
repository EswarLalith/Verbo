How this works

Learn more about the features and functionality of this application.

Step 1: Audio Transcription

Click the microphone button and speak into it. The application will transcribe your speech into text.

Step 2: Text Transcription

If you prefer to type, you can enter your text into the provided textarea. The application will process your input.

Step 3: Token optimized Text

After transcription, the application will optimize your text using Token optimization feature, improving its effectiveness and efficiency.
